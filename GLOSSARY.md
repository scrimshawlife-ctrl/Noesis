# Glossary

## Alignment map
A fitted transformation relating two representation spaces. It is an experimental artifact with scope limits, not proof of semantic identity.

## CANON-SHADOW
A project posture in which evidence may be collected, compared, and reviewed but is not promoted into canonical Abraxas doctrine or runtime behavior.

## Candidate feature
A latent direction, sparse feature, cluster, or other representation component proposed for further testing.

## Causal evidence
Evidence from intervention such as activation patching, ablation, steering, or controlled injection that changes an outcome in the predicted way.

## Feature dictionary
A versioned decomposition of model activations, including a sparse autoencoder or equivalent learned representation.

## Hyperlexical
The sibling lexical/symbolic module responsible for surface language, register, slang, semantic transformations, and related metadata used by Noesis experiments.

## Invariance
Measured stability of a representation under a specified transformation set and metric. Invariance is always scoped to model, site, data, transforms, and tolerance.

## Latent communication
Direct transfer of continuous learned representations between computational agents/models without requiring the message to be fully rendered as natural-language tokens.

## Neuralese
Informal research shorthand for machine-native latent/continuous representations. In Noesis this is **not** a canonical claim that models possess a universal hidden natural language.

## Noesis
Abraxas research module for latent-representation measurement, probing, falsification, alignment, causal experimentation, and settlement.

## Observation
An immutable record of a captured representation and its provenance. Observations do not contain semantic conclusions.

## Probe
A model or statistic used to test whether information can be recovered from a representation. Probe success does not by itself prove that the probed variable is causally used.

## Representation site
The exact model component and layer/location from which an embedding, hidden state, residual state, attention value, MLP value, or custom activation is captured.

## Settlement
A durable evidence-backed proposition with provenance label, limitations, contradictions, and promotion recommendation.

## SHADOW
Anomaly/drift/contamination detection lane. It does not perform predictive inference.

## FORECAST
Evidence-based inference lane operating on defined evidence. It does not convert raw anomalies directly into predictions.

## Surface transformation
A controlled change in lexical form, register, language, metaphor, symbolism, or syntax with a declared expectation about whether semantics should be preserved or altered.
