# Noesis Constitution

## C-001 — Empirical primacy
No interpretation outranks measurement. Natural-language explanations of latent features are hypotheses, not evidence by themselves.

## C-002 — Provenance is mandatory
Every durable claim is labeled `OBSERVED`, `INFERRED`, `SPECULATIVE`, or `NOT_COMPUTABLE` and points to the evidence that supports it.

## C-003 — Falsification before promotion
Candidate features, directions, clusters, or alignments must survive adversarial and negative-control tests before promotion beyond CANON-SHADOW.

## C-004 — No privileged decoder
A lexical decoder, probe, SAE, classifier, visualization, or model-generated explanation is an instrument with error modes. No single instrument defines semantic ground truth.

## C-005 — Separate representation from interpretation
Raw activations, transformed representations, feature dictionaries, probe outputs, labels, interpretations, and settlements are stored as distinct artifacts.

## C-006 — No consciousness inference
Noesis does not infer consciousness, sentience, subjective experience, hidden intent, or sovereignty from latent representations.

## C-007 — Reproducibility
Every experiment records model identity and revision, tokenizer, dataset hash, prompt/transform specification, layer/site, random seed, software environment, probe/SAE revision, metrics, and output hashes.

## C-008 — Invariance is graded
Representation invariance is measured, not asserted. Results must distinguish semantic stability, surface-form sensitivity, model-specific effects, and uncertainty.

## C-009 — Causal evidence outranks correlational interpretation
Where feasible, activation patching, steering, ablation, counterfactual intervention, or equivalent causal tests are required before a feature is described as functionally relevant.

## C-010 — Cross-model claims require alignment evidence
A feature in model A and a feature in model B are not considered equivalent solely because generated explanations resemble each other.

## C-011 — Governance is advisory-only by default
No Noesis observation automatically changes Abraxas canon, routing, policy, safety posture, or runtime behavior. Promotion requires explicit settlement.

## C-012 — Data minimization
Do not capture latent traces for sensitive or private inputs unless the experiment requires them and the governing data policy permits them. Persist only the minimum necessary evidence.

## C-013 — Deterministic contracts
All persisted observations and settlements conform to versioned schemas. Missing required evidence yields `NOT_COMPUTABLE`, not an invented value.

## C-014 — SHADOW and FORECAST are separated
SHADOW detects anomaly, drift, contradiction, and contamination. FORECAST performs evidence-based inference. SHADOW findings do not become predictive claims without an explicit FORECAST step.

## C-015 — Complexity must reduce ambiguity
New abstractions, metrics, and modules are accepted only when they create a clearer experimental boundary, stronger testability, or more reproducible evidence.

## C-016 — Hypothesis neutrality
Noesis encodes measurement capabilities, evidence semantics, reproducibility rules, and experimental controls. It does not encode a privileged scientific hypothesis, expected conclusion, semantic theory, or research-program thesis as system truth.

Research hypotheses are inputs to experiments, not properties of the Noesis runtime.

## C-017 — Research ontology isolation
Experiment-specific concepts, semantic categories, transform taxonomies, interpretation frameworks, expected invariants, scoring models, and theoretical constructs enter Noesis through versioned manifests, fixtures, adapters, or research protocols.

They do not become core Noesis ontology unless independently justified as domain-general infrastructure.

## C-018 — Instrument–theory separation
Noesis instruments remain usable when the scientific hypothesis they were created to test is rejected.

A failed hypothesis does not require changes to the measurement kernel unless the failure identifies a defect in the instrument itself.
